# Verified foundational papers, framework docs, platform policies and industry write-ups for an AI pentest / bug-bounty hackbot (checked 2026-10-27)

**How items were checked, and what the tags mean.** From this sandbox, arxiv.org, export.arxiv.org, Semantic Scholar, openai.com, policies.google.com, hackerone.com, bugcrowd.com, xbow.com, Project Zero and most blogs could not be fetched (proxy 403 or DNS failure, for both curl and WebFetch). The hosts that could be reached were raw.githubusercontent.com, www.anthropic.com, pypi.org and registry.npmjs.org. So:
- **[V]** means one of three things. (a) The authors' official GitHub README or BibTeX was fetched directly. (b) The primary page was fetched directly (anthropic.com, or a blog's source repo). (c) A search result from the primary domain itself (arxiv.org, openai.com, xbow.com and so on) showed the exact title and URL. In case (c), the date and summary details come from the search tool's extract of that primary page and were not read in full. That is noted wherever it matters.
- **[V2]** means a secondary source only: citing papers, press, aggregators, or a search summary with no primary-domain hit for that detail.
- The arXiv ID prefix (YYMM) fixes the month of v1 submission. Exact days come from arXiv listings via search.
- About 62 web searches were used.

### Inferences
- The offensive-security papers form one lineage: NYU CTF Bench → EnIGMA → D-CIPHER → CRAKEN (shared NYU/Princeton authors Shao, Udeshi, Dolan-Gavitt and Karri). Cybench → BountyBench (Stanford, Andy K. Zhang) is a second one. Citing both lineages makes the benchmark story coherent.
- Because EnIGMA, D-CIPHER, Cybench and Greshake all changed titles between arXiv versions, the report should cite each by arXiv ID plus its current title and avoid old BibTeX copied from repos.
- ARTEMIS (an 82% valid-submission rate, with false positives noted) and EnIGMA's "soliloquizing" both support a hackbot design with an independent validator and grounded-observation checks.

### Gaps
- Full author lists were not seen on primary pages for CoT (only "Wei, Wang, Schuurmans + 7"), OpenHands, Cybench, NYU CTF Bench, BountyBench, CRAKEN and ARTEMIS. Cite these as "First Author et al.".
- Venues not confirmed from a primary source: Toolformer (NeurIPS 2023), NYU CTF Bench (NeurIPS 2024 D&B), EnIGMA (ICML 2025), PenHeal (2023 vs 2024), ToT and debate (no venue checked), and Lost in the Middle (believed TACL 2024, unverified).
